# Visual reference: match this look

**Reference:** JC Lomibao, "Portfolio (Graphic Design & Marketing) 2026" on Behance
https://www.behance.net/gallery/252536285/Portfolio-(Graphic-Design-Marketing)-2026
(Behance may block automated access. The screenshots in this folder show it.)

Geetika wants her site to look visually like this reference: the same visual language, layout system, level of detail and polish. Use her own content, colours and objects. Don't copy JC's text, logo, photos, his face or his specific artworks.

## What the reference does (match all of this)

1. **Computer desktop metaphor everywhere.** The whole page sits inside a Mac-style app window (title bar with red, yellow and green dots and the owner's name). Blue folder icons with labels float on the cover. Galleries are Finder-style windows ("Contents", sidebar "JC's Files", grid/list icons) with images labelled like files (`aliens.jpg`, `hero.jpg`). (01, 04, 07)
2. **Cream dot-grid background** across the whole page, like a design canvas. (all)
3. **Collage cover.** A ransom-note style "Portfolio" title where every letter is a different typeface or material (pixel, gradient, script, fuzzy fur, plain bold). Real photo cutouts with drop shadows (dragonflies, clownfish, a grayscale face cutout with a camera focus box). A torn-paper edge revealing a real water photo behind, with a transparent checkerboard showing at the tear. (01, 02)
4. **Pill tags** in the signature colour ("graphic design", "marketing", "smm") and real app icons (Ps, Ai, Pr) on the cover. (02)
5. **Folder-tab section dividers.** Each section opens with a full-width solid folder in the signature colour, a tab on top with the section number, and a huge wide uppercase title in cream ("TYPOGRAPHY", "BRANDING"). (05, 08)
6. **Playful UI details:** pixel-art cursors and sparkles, a Photoshop toolbar, a colour-picker window, toggle switches as bullet points, a dashed path, a selection box with handles. (03, 04, 05, 06)
7. **About** = a cut-out head with pixel sparkles, a justified paragraph with key roles in the accent colour, then Education, Skills (yellow check icons) and Experience in two columns, plus a "Tools" app-icon tray. (03, 04)
8. **Brand board as stacked app windows**, each window titled ("brand icon", "brand pattern", "brand typography", "brand social media page"), overlapping on the dot grid with real mockups (an apron, a sticker) beside them. (09)
9. **Merch as shop cards:** grey rounded cards, real photo mockups on models, product name, a heart icon. (10)
10. **One signature colour** (electric blue) with cream and black. Everything else comes from the photos.

## Geetika's changes (these override the reference)

- **Colours:** bright pastels. Light blue and light pink lead, with butter yellow, mint, lilac and peach. Her signature colour replaces the electric blue on folder dividers, pill tags and accents. Pick one bold, saturated pastel-world colour (e.g. a strong pink or a strong sky blue) so it still pops like the reference.
- **Name small.** No giant name. The window title bar can say "Geetika Agarwal", and the ransom-note title says "Portfolio", like the reference.
- **No photo or cut-out of her face.** Replace JC's face cutout in About with a collage object (camera, passport or polaroid).
- **Collage objects:** camera, clapperboard, event tickets and wristbands, momos, travel items (passport, boarding pass, luggage tag, plane), coins and rupee notes. Use the PNG cutouts in /assets/cutouts.
- **Motion:** the cover folder opens as you scroll and items slide out sideways. Sections move sideways as you scroll into them. The folder-tab dividers can slide in from the side.
- **Content and sections:** as in CLAUDE.md (CRFTD, Too Yumm!, Tiya reels, AquaTerra, media planning, PR desk, photography, also on my desk, contact).
- **Section worlds, in the reference's style:** CRFTD = merch shop cards like screenshot 10; Too Yumm = phone mockups inside app windows; AquaTerra = posters in a file-browser window like 07; media planning = charts inside stacked app windows like 09; PR = the press release as a newspaper-style file like the "NEWS DAILY" piece in 07; photography = file-browser gallery with `.jpg` labels.
